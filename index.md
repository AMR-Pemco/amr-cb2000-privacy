CB-2000 BT - Privacy Policy
Effective date: 2026-11-20
American Mine Research ("AMR", "we") publishes the CB-2000 BT Android application ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.
What the app does
The app connects over Bluetooth Low Energy to AMR CB-2000 fused/contactor circuit breakers to display live readings, fault history, and to change breaker settings. It is a tool for installers and maintenance staff.
Information the app collects
Diagnostics and crash reports. The app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring and Firebase Analytics. These services collect crash logs, performance traces, app version, device model, operating system version, a per-install identifier generated by Firebase, and anonymised usage events (for example which screens are opened). This data is used only to find and fix defects and to understand how the app performs. It is transmitted to Google and processed under Google's terms. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
Device identifier. The app attaches the Android ID to crash reports so that repeated crashes from the same installation can be grouped. It is not used for advertising or shared with anyone other than Google Firebase as part of the crash report.
Saved device list. The app stores, on the phone only, the names and Bluetooth addresses of CB-2000 breakers found by a scan, so they can be shown under "Saved Devices". This list never leaves the phone.
Saved files. When you save a graph or the fault history, the app writes a PDF to the phone's Downloads/CB2000 folder. The app also keeps automatic backups of the live readings and fault history as CSV and PDF files in Downloads/CB2000/backups, and saves a PDF of the last 24 hours of faults at midnight while it is running. These files stay on the phone. When you choose Share, the file is handed to the Android share sheet, and where it goes is entirely your choice.
Notifications. Fault and status notifications are created on the phone from data the breaker sends. They are not sent to AMR or to any server.
Information the app does not collect
The app does not collect or transmit your location, and it does not request the location permission. Bluetooth scanning is used only to find CB-2000 breakers.
The app does not require an account, and does not collect names, email addresses, contacts or photos.
The app does not show advertising and does not sell or share data with third parties for marketing.
Breaker readings, fault history, and device configuration stay between the phone and the CB-2000 breaker, apart from the files you save or share yourself. They are not sent to AMR or to any server.
Permissions
Bluetooth (scan and connect): to find and talk to CB-2000 breakers
Notifications: to show breaker fault alerts and that monitoring is running
Foreground service (connected device) and wake lock: to stay connected to the breaker while the app is in the background
Ignore battery optimizations: so Android does not stop the connection during long monitoring sessions
Internet and network state: used only by the Firebase diagnostics described above
Data retention and deletion
Crash and analytics data is retained by Firebase according to Google's retention settings (crash data up to 90 days, analytics up to 14 months) and is then deleted. Uninstalling the app removes the saved device list. Files saved to the Downloads folder stay until you delete them. To request deletion of diagnostics tied to your installation, contact us at the address below with your device model and approximate install date.
Children
The app is not directed at children and is intended for professional use.
